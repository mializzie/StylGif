How It Works

01

Choose Your Palette

Pick from curated pixel color palettes or build your own. Every color you choose gets baked into the animation.

02

Draw Frame by Frame

Paint on the 16×16 pixel canvas. Add as many frames as you need — each frame becomes a step in the animation cycle.

03

Hit save and choose to export your frames as-is, or let AI bring your drawing to life — a butterfly flapping, a frog hopping, a face smiling — then download the looping GIF.
